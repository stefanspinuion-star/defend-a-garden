About the game

Far out in a warm sea grows a garden that grows too fast. Old Fennick kept it for years, until one autumn night he lit every lantern and was gone. The player is the new Keeper.

By day you garden; every night the Riot comes: critters race down the path for the garden's glowing Heart, and you hold the night with awakened defenders and Fennick's old tools. Underneath the cosy surface is a mystery about what the garden takes from the island — told in fragments that every player finds in a different order.

Key features

  • 6 defenders, 3 weapons with skills, bosses every 5th night
  • 37 story secrets around the island and a night sky that tracks them
  • Day and night cycle: the sunset is the warning, the Riot is midnight
  • Weekly hunts and a shared goal: a bridge of roots that all Keepers grow together
  • Every purchase has a free way to the same thing
